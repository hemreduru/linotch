# Third-party assets

`claude.png` and `openai.png` are single-colour renders of the corresponding SVGs
from [lobe-icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons) (MIT), used unmodified in
shape. They are drawn as alpha masks, so the colour in the app is applied by
`src/icons.rs` rather than baked into the file.

The marks remain the trademarks of their respective owners. They identify which
account a ring reports on; they do not imply endorsement.
